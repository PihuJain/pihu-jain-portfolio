ChokeHelm.

THE IDEA

Find a safer operating point

ChokeHelm chooses hourly choke settings to balance output and pressure constraints using a predictive controller. Its project notes report 120 randomized plant simulations with zero safety violations.

The work explores how control logic can be evaluated under varied operating conditions before deployment.

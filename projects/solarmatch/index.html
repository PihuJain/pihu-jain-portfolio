PROJECT 05 · CLEAN ENERGY

SolarMatch.

A machine learning experiment forecasting next-day surplus rooftop solar generation to support peer-to-peer energy trading.

Pythonscikit-learnRegressionTime-series validation
THE IDEA

Help match solar surplus with local demand

SolarMatch uses weather and load data to estimate the next day’s rooftop solar surplus. The project explores regression models and time-series cross-validation for a potential peer-to-peer energy trading use case.
